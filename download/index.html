System requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Any inkjet or laser printer that can feed a cheque leaf (rear tray or manual feed recommended)
  • About 300 MB free disk space

Install

  1. Download the installer and run it, or unzip the portable version anywhere and run Cheque Printer.exe.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. This appears for new apps that are not yet code-signed. TODO: remove once the app is code-signed
  3. Open the app, choose your bank and printer orientation, and follow the printing and calibration guide.

Safety

Only download Cheque Printer from this page. The app does not need internet access to print and does not upload your cheque data. This site is not a browser-based printer: online cheque printer and cheque printing software explain why, and how to print a cheque walks through home and office use.

Cheque Printer

Cheque Printer is free Windows software that prints Indian bank cheques on CTS-2010 leaves (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Saraswat and more) with amount in words in lakh/crore, A/C Payee crossing and per-field calibration.
