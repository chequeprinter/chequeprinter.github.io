Everything you need to print cheques correctly

Cheque Printer is a free desktop app for Windows that prints on pre-printed Indian bank cheque leaves. You type the details once; it places every field exactly where your bank's CTS-2010 cheque expects it.

CTS-2010 layout

Built around the RBI CTS-2010 cheque format: 8 date boxes (DDMMYYYY), payee line, two amount-in-words lines and the amount box. What is CTS-2010?

Indian bank templates

SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Saraswat and more, plus a standard CTS template for other banks. Supported banks

Amount in words: lakh & crore

Correct Indian-format words with "Only", split across the two lines. Try the converter

A/C Payee crossing

Two parallel lines with "A/C PAYEE ONLY", printed in full in the top-left corner without moving other text.

Horizontal and vertical feed

Works whether your printer takes the cheque landscape or end-first through a rear/manual tray.

Per-field calibration

Nudge date, payee, words and figures in 0.25-1 mm steps; a change in one orientation is applied to the other automatically. Reset to default any time.

Print a cheque in 4 steps

  1. Choose your bank and feed orientation (horizontal or vertical).
  2. Type the payee, date and amount. The amount in words is filled in automatically.
  3. Tick A/C Payee if you want the crossing.
  4. Print at A4, scale 100%, margins none. Fine-tune once with a test print on plain paper.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cheque Printer free?

Yes. Cheque Printer is free to download and use, with no ads. Development is supported by voluntary donations.

Which banks does Cheque Printer support?

It includes templates for Indian CTS-2010 cheques such as SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and Saraswat Bank, and a standard CTS-2010 layout that works for other Indian banks after a quick calibration.

Does it write the amount in words in lakh and crore?

Yes. Amounts are written in the Indian numbering system, for example 'Rupees Twelve Lakh Thirty Four Thousand Five Hundred Only'.

Which printers work?

Any inkjet or laser printer that can feed a cheque leaf, either horizontally or vertically (for example through a rear or manual feed tray). Print at 100% scale with no margins.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Cheque Printer is a desktop app that runs offline; your payees and settings stay on your PC.
