Donate: support Cheque Printer development

Cheque Printer is free and has no ads or paid tiers in the app. Donations pay for development time, testing on real cheque leaves, printers for calibration, code signing and hosting. Choose UPI (no gateway fee) or Razorpay (card, netbanking or wallet).

Pay directly by UPI (0% fee)

Scan the QR with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app, or open your UPI app from a phone. The payment goes straight to the Cheque Printer UPI ID, with no gateway fee.

UPI ID: BHARATPE.8L0W0K4N8O74197@fbpe

Pay with UPI app

On a phone, “Pay with UPI app” opens GPay, PhonePe or Paytm with the amount left for you to enter. On a computer, scan the QR. The name shown in your UPI app is the name registered on the UPI ID.

Pay by card / netbanking / wallet (Razorpay)

Use a debit or credit card, netbanking, a wallet, or UPI through Razorpay if you prefer a checkout receipt. Razorpay charges its standard fee on the payment.

The checkout opens on Razorpay’s secure page. We receive only the payment confirmation they share with us (such as your name and the amount).

Where your donation goes

  • New and better bank templates (each needs real test prints).
  • Bug fixes and Windows updates.
  • A code-signing certificate so Windows trusts the installer.
  • Website and download hosting.

Other ways to help

  • Tell other businesses and accountants about Cheque Printer.
  • Send a scan of a cancelled cheque leaf (account details hidden) for a bank we don't have yet.
  • Report problems via the contact page.

Cheque Printer

Cheque Printer is free Windows software that prints Indian bank cheques on CTS-2010 leaves (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Saraswat and more) with amount in words in lakh/crore, A/C Payee crossing and per-field calibration.
