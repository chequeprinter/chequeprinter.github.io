Rules for amount in words on Indian cheques

  • Start with "Rupees" (often pre-printed) and end with "Only".
  • Use lakh and crore, not million and billion.
  • Don't leave gaps between words, so nothing can be added.
  • The words and the figures must match; if they differ, banks may return the cheque.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write 1,50,000 in words on a cheque?

Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only.

How do I write 10,00,000 in words?

Rupees Ten Lakh Only.

How do I write paise on a cheque?

Add 'and ... Paise' before 'Only', for example 'Rupees One Thousand Two Hundred and Fifty Paise Only' for 1,200.50.

Should I write 'Lakh' or 'Lakhs'?

Both are commonly accepted; 'Lakh' is standard in Indian English. Be consistent and end with 'Only'.
