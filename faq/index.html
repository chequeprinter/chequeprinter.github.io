Cheque Printer FAQ

General

Is Cheque Printer free?

Yes. Cheque Printer is free to download and use, with no ads. Development is supported by voluntary donations.

Which banks does Cheque Printer support?

It includes templates for Indian CTS-2010 cheques such as SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and Saraswat Bank, and a standard CTS-2010 layout that works for other Indian banks after a quick calibration.

Does it write the amount in words in lakh and crore?

Yes. Amounts are written in the Indian numbering system, for example 'Rupees Twelve Lakh Thirty Four Thousand Five Hundred Only'.

Which printers work?

Any inkjet or laser printer that can feed a cheque leaf, either horizontally or vertically (for example through a rear or manual feed tray). Print at 100% scale with no margins.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Cheque Printer is a desktop app that runs offline; your payees and settings stay on your PC.

Who makes Cheque Printer?

Cheque Printer is an independent project built in India, supported by donations from users.

Is there a web version?

No public web app prints cheques in the browser. Printing is done in the Cheque Printer desktop app for Windows, which you download from this site. Your cheque details stay on your PC.

Printing and alignment

Why is the print shifted on my cheque?

Check the print dialog: A4, scale 100% (not Fit), margins none, headers and footers off. Then calibrate the printer offset once for your feed orientation.

What is horizontal vs vertical feed?

Horizontal means the cheque goes into the printer in landscape, long edge first. Vertical means it goes in end-first (portrait), common with rear or manual feed trays.

If I adjust a field in horizontal mode, do I have to redo vertical?

No. Field adjustments are made in cheque coordinates and are applied to both orientations. Only the printer offset is separate per orientation.

Can I reset my adjustments?

Yes. Your changes are stored separately from the built-in defaults, and each field can be reset to default.

The A/C Payee crossing is cut off. What do I do?

Update to the latest version: the crossing is printed at a fixed spot in the top-left corner so field shifts can't push it off the leaf.

Cheques in India

What does A/C Payee mean on a cheque?

An 'A/C Payee' (account payee) crossing instructs the bank to credit the amount only to the bank account of the named payee; it cannot be cashed over the counter.

Can I print the signature?

No. Cheque Printer does not print signatures; sign the cheque by hand.

Is a printed cheque valid?

Yes. Banks accept cheques filled by printer, provided the leaf is a genuine CTS-2010 leaf from your bank, the details are correct and it is signed by hand.
