Print settings (important)

  1. Press Print cheque. In the print dialog choose your printer.
  2. Paper size: the same as on this page: A4, or 8 × 7 in if you created that custom size in the printer driver. Chrome and Edge take the size from the page; Firefox and Safari may need it set by hand.
  3. Scale 100% (Actual size / Default). Never “Fit to page” or “Shrink to fit”.
  4. Margins: None, Headers and footers: off, Background graphics: off.
  5. Put the cheque face up, right side down (date end first) against the guide you picked. Print once on plain paper, hold it over a cheque leaf, and use the X/Y fine-tune if needed.

Prefer an app? The free Windows app has the same layout plus per-bank calibration, saved payees and a calibration test page. See also how to print and calibrate.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print a cheque from the browser?

Yes. Open the Print online page, choose your bank, type the date, payee and amount, and press Print. The page fills the amount in words (lakh/crore) and places every field for a CTS-2010 leaf fed right side down. Nothing you type leaves your computer.

Which paper size should I choose?

A4 works on every browser and printer and is the default. If your printer driver has a custom 8 × 7 inch paper size, choose 8 × 7 in on the page and in the print dialog; Chrome and Edge pick it up from the page automatically. Either way the cheque prints in the same physical place.

How do I put the cheque in the printer?

Face up, portrait, right side down: the date end of the cheque goes into the printer first, so the date prints first. Slide it against the guide you chose on the page (centre for most Canon and Epson rear trays).

What print settings are needed?

Scale 100% (or Actual size / Default, never Fit to page), Margins None, Headers and footers off, Background graphics off. A wrong scale moves every field.

Why is the date also printed at the bottom?

Some leaves have date boxes that print poorly. The remark line at the bottom centre prints 'Date: DD-MM-YYYY' by default; you can change or clear it, and turn the date boxes off. A/C Payee Only is printed as plain text next to the remark so it never touches the payee name.

Is my cheque data stored or uploaded?

No. The page runs entirely in your browser. Only your calibration (bank, paper, shifts) is saved in this browser's local storage; payee and amount are never saved or sent.
