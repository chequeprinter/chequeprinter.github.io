Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20. TODO(legal): confirm against the app's actual behaviour (e.g. update checks, crash reporting) before publishing.

The app

Cheque Printer runs on your Windows PC. Payee names, amounts, dates and your calibration settings are stored locally on your computer and are not sent to us. The app needs no account.

This website

This website is a static information and download site. It is hosted as ordinary HTML files (for example on GitHub Pages). Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for security and reliability. This site does not include the cheque-printing application and does not collect payee names, amounts or account details.

Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

Some article and tool pages may show advertisements served by Google AdSense, once a publisher ID has been configured and you have accepted cookies. Until you accept, advertising cookies stay off (Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied). If you reject, we do not load the ad script.

If you accept, Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads, measure how they perform, and (if you allow personalisation) tailor ads. Google and its partners may collect data such as cookie identifiers, IP address, and information about the pages you visit on this site. We do not combine that with cheque or payment data from the desktop app, because the app runs on your PC and is not this website.

  • Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • You can change your mind by clearing this site’s cookies (the consent banner will appear again) or by using Google Ad Settings and, where available, industry opt-outs such as Your Online Choices.
  • Ads are not placed on the home, download or donate pages, or on the main “online cheque printer”, “print cheque” and “cheque printing software” landing pages.

If AdSense has not been configured yet, this site sets no advertising cookies and shows no ad slots.

Google Analytics

Once a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID has been configured, this website may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visits (pages viewed, country, device type, and events such as donate or download clicks). Analytics cookies and identifiers stay off until you accept them in the cookie banner (Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied). If you reject, we do not use analytics cookies. If Analytics has not been configured yet, no Analytics script is loaded.

Google’s own privacy policy applies to data Google processes: Google Privacy Policy. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Donations

You can donate by UPI, which opens your UPI app or uses the QR code on the donate page, or through Razorpay’s checkout (card, netbanking, wallet or UPI). UPI payments go directly to the published UPI ID. Razorpay payments are processed by Razorpay; their terms and privacy policy apply to that checkout, and we receive only the confirmation they share with us (such as your name and the amount). We do not collect card numbers.

Cheque Printer

Cheque Printer is free Windows software that prints Indian bank cheques on CTS-2010 leaves (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Saraswat and more) with amount in words in lakh/crore, A/C Payee crossing and per-field calibration.
