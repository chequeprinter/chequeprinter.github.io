How to print a cheque on your printer and calibrate it

Print SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis or Saraswat cheques neatly on your own printer, without wasting leaves.

Download freeHow to print

Free · Windows 10/11 · Works offline

Short answer: pick your bank and feed orientation, type the payee, date and amount, print a test on plain paper at A4 / 100% / no margins, correct any field by a few millimetres, then print on the real cheque leaf.

New to this? Start with how to print a cheque at home or in the office. This page covers print-dialog settings and calibration.

Step-by-step

  1. Install and open Cheque Printer. download it for Windows and start the app.
  2. Select your bank. choose SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Saraswat or another bank's template.
  3. Select the feed orientation. horizontal if the cheque goes in landscape, vertical if it goes in end-first.
  4. Enter cheque details. date, payee name and amount in figures; the amount in words is generated automatically.
  5. Choose A/C Payee if needed. tick the crossing option for account-payee cheques.
  6. Test print on plain paper. print at A4, scale 100%, margins none, then hold the print over a real cheque leaf against light.
  7. Calibrate. move any field that is off by entering a millimetre correction; repeat the test until it matches.
  8. Print on the cheque leaf. insert the leaf the same way as the test sheet and print.

How calibration works

There are two layers of adjustment:

  • Printer offset (per orientation): moves everything together, to correct how your printer feeds paper. Set this once.
  • Field offsets: move one field (date, payee, words line 1, words line 2, amount box). A field correction made in horizontal mode is applied to vertical mode too, because the field sits in the same place on the cheque.

Built-in defaults are never overwritten: your changes are stored separately, and each field has a reset to default.

Tips to avoid wasting cheque leaves

  • Always test on plain A4 first and compare against a cheque leaf held up to light.
  • Measure the error with a ruler in millimetres; change one field at a time.
  • Keep the leaf straight and against the guide; skew causes more error than any setting.
  • Use permanent black ink and don't touch the print until dry.
  • Never print or write over the MICR band at the bottom. Why

Frequently asked questions

Why does my cheque print shifted or smaller?

Usually the print dialog is scaling the page. Choose A4, scale 100% (not 'Fit to page') and margins none, and turn off headers and footers in browsers.

How do I feed a cheque into my printer?

Use the rear or manual feed tray if your printer has one. Note the orientation (landscape = horizontal, end-first = vertical) and whether the printer centres the paper, and select the same orientation in the app.

Should I calibrate for every bank?

Calibrate the printer once per orientation. Bank templates handle the differences between banks; fine-tune a bank only if its leaf design is different.

Can I use laser or inkjet?

Both work. Use permanent (pigment or toner) black ink, which resists smudging and water better than dye ink.
