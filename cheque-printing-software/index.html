What cheque printing software actually does

Cheque print software does not create a new kind of cheque. Your bank still issues the paper. The software’s job is to put the variable text — date, payee, amount in words, amount in figures, and often an A/C Payee crossing — in the printed boxes and lines of that leaf, using your inkjet or laser printer.

That is different from payroll software, a core-banking printout, or a “design a blank cheque” template in a word processor. A word-processor template almost never matches a real CTS-2010 leaf, and printing a cheque-shaped picture on plain paper is not a valid cheque. Dedicated cheque printing software is built around the leaves banks already give you.

What to look for in cheque printing software in India

If you compare products, these are the points that decide whether a cheque will clear smoothly.

  • CTS-2010 layout. Indian cheques used in clearing follow the Reserve Bank of India’s CTS-2010 standard: a leaf of about 202 × 92 mm, eight date boxes, a clear MICR band, and tight rules on alterations. Software that ignores this will misplace fields. Read our CTS-2010 summary.
  • Indian amount in words. 1,50,000 must become “Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only”, not “one hundred and fifty thousand”. Million-based wording looks wrong on an Indian cheque and can cause delays. Try our free converter.
  • Bank coverage. You want usable results on SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and the other banks you actually use, including co-operative banks that still issue CTS leaves. See supported banks.
  • Alignment on your printer. Every printer feeds paper a little differently. Good software lets you nudge fields so a date digit sits inside its box on your machine, without you having to invent a layout.
  • A/C Payee crossing printed fully, not clipped, and not at the expense of the payee line.
  • Horizontal and vertical feed, because home ink-tank printers often take a cheque end-first through a rear tray.
  • Offline use, no forced account. Payee names and amounts are financial data. Prefer software that stores them on your PC and does not require an online login to print.
  • A clear price. Free cheque printing software is enough for many users. If you pay, you should know what extra you are getting.
