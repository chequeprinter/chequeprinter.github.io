SBI cheque printing format

The SBI template was tuned with test prints on an actual SBI CTS-2010 cheque. One difference we found versus several other leaves: the first line of the amount in words starts noticeably further to the right on SBI cheques (about 10 mm compared with the Saraswat leaf), because the pre-printed 'Rupees' label is placed differently. The template accounts for this.

Fields printed on a SBI cheque:

  • Date in the 8 boxes at the top right, as DDMMYYYY.
  • Payee name on the "Pay" line.
  • Amount in words on the "Rupees" lines, in lakh/crore format, ending with "Only".
  • Amount in figures in the ₹ box, e.g. 2,50,000.00 in Indian digit grouping.
  • A/C Payee crossing (optional) in the top-left corner.

The signature, and anything in the MICR band at the bottom, are never printed.

How to print a SBI cheque

  1. Select State Bank of India (SBI) and your feed orientation.
  2. Enter date, payee and amount; check the words.
  3. Test on plain paper at A4 / 100% / no margins.
  4. Fine-tune if needed, then print on the leaf.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print SBI cheques on my printer?

Yes. Choose State Bank of India (SBI) in Cheque Printer, enter the details, and print at A4, 100% scale, no margins. Do one plain-paper test print first.

Is the SBI cheque format CTS-2010?

State Bank of India (SBI) issues CTS-2010 compliant cheques, like all banks in India's cheque truncation system. Look for 'CTS-2010' printed on the leaf.

How do I write the amount in words on a SBI cheque?

Write it in the Indian system (lakh, crore) ending with 'Only', for example 'Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Only'. Cheque Printer does this automatically.

Why is my SBI cheque print slightly off?

Printers feed paper slightly differently. Use the per-field calibration to move the date, payee or amount by a millimetre at a time; the correction is saved for this bank.
