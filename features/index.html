Features

Cheque Printer features

Everything needed to print Indian bank cheques neatly and in the right place, on any printer.

Download freeHow to print

Free · Windows 10/11 · Works offline

CTS-2010 cheque layout

Fields positioned for RBI's CTS-2010 cheque format (about 202 × 92 mm): date boxes, payee, amount in words (2 lines), amount in figures.

Indian bank templates

Calibrated templates for Saraswat Bank and SBI; HDFC, ICICI, Axis and other banks use a tested standard CTS layout that you can fine-tune.

Amount in words (lakh/crore)

Indian numbering with 'Rupees ... Only' and paise; automatic split across the two words lines.

Date in DDMMYYYY boxes

Each digit lands in its own box, as CTS cheques require.

A/C Payee crossing

Optional crossing with two parallel lines and 'A/C PAYEE ONLY', printed in full near the top-left corner.

Horizontal and vertical feed

Separate printer offsets for landscape feed and end-first (vertical) feed through a rear or manual tray.

Per-field calibration

Move date, payee, both words lines and the amount box independently in millimetres.

System defaults + your adjustments

Built-in defaults stay intact; your tweaks are saved separately and can be reset per field.

Orientation sync

Adjust a field in horizontal mode and the same correction applies to vertical (and vice versa).

Print preview

See exactly what will print before you use a real cheque leaf.

Overseas templates

Cheque formats for the US, Canada, UK and Australia. Details

Offline and private

Runs on your PC; no account, no upload of payee or amount data.
